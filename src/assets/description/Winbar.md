## Winbar
An extensible, module-based utility bar for Windows built with Python and the PySide6 framework, customizable visa custom python modules and organized using JSON configurations.

![Winbar Demo](./Winbar%20Demo.mp4)
![Winbar Picture](./Winbar.png)

### Why did I bother building this?
Honestly, I just really liked the idea of floating utility bars; I watched a couple videos of people customizing hyprland and the difference in the way the utility bars looked compared to the Windows task bar was the main reason I built this.

Now you could say: just use hyprland.

That's a good point, but isn't this more fun? :)

As much as I like the Windows default taskbar as the next person, I kind of really hate that there's no way to deeply customize it - the most you can do is change the colors or hide some icons. By building my own utility bar I just get full control over all of that and based on the colors on the above examples, I can completely tone it to the theme of my background!
