### Why did I structure the website the way I did?
You might be wondering why there are different team logos on the bottom left of the screen, and if it wasn't very apparent up to this point, I'm a very passionate football (⚽) fan; I watch games from all over the world, the famous ones, the 3rd division of Japan, the Azerbaijani Premier League... if there's even a single interesting player you bet I'm watching it. 

All of that resulted in me supporting teams from all around the world. I just thought it'd be a cool effect to see the different color palettes of the different teams I support organized by the amount of time I've supported them!
